Privacy Policy.

What we collect, why we collect it and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

tradewithalix is run by Arshad Ali ("Alix", "we"). This policy explains what personal information this site collects, why we collect it and what choices you have. You can reach us any time through the Contact page.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your first name, last name and email address. Members sign in with a one-time code sent by email, so we do not keep a member password. Sign-in codes are stored only as hashes and expire after 15 minutes.
  • Messages and updates: the name, email and optional message you send through our forms. We email you a confirmation when you send one.
  • Trading account link (optional): if you send a broker name and account number, we keep them so Alix can review your request.
  • Payments: payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive your plan, the amount, the order and payment IDs and whether the payment worked. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • On your device: a sign-in token and your theme choice are kept in your browser storage so the site can remember you.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may log your IP address and basic request details for security and to keep the site running.

3. How we use it

  • To create and manage your account and give you access to member notes.
  • To process payments and keep records of them.
  • To reply to your messages and send the updates you asked for.
  • To keep the site secure and fix problems.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run the site: Razorpay for payments, the email service that delivers sign-in codes and confirmations, our hosting and database providers (currently Render and MongoDB Atlas), and Google Fonts, which serves the fonts and can see your IP address when they load. They may only use your data to provide their service. We may also share information when the law requires it.

5. Links and embeds

This site links to Telegram, YouTube, Instagram, brokers and other services. Their privacy practices are their own, so please read their policies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account and payment records for as long as your account is active and for as long as we need them for our records or to meet legal obligations. Messages are kept for as long as we need them to deal with your request.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including short-lived sign-in codes stored as hashes and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information through the Contact page.
  • Ask us to stop sending updates at any time.
  • Log out, or clear your browser storage, to remove the sign-in token from your device.

9. Children

This site is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please do not create an account or send us your information.
