High risk

Trading foreign exchange and CFDs on margin is high risk and is not suitable for everyone. Prices can move fast and against you. You can lose some or all of your money and, depending on your account's terms, more than you deposit. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.

Leverage

Leverage lets you control a large position with a small deposit. It magnifies losses as well as gains.

Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. Any results, screenshots or statistics on this site show what happened in one account over one period. They are not a promise of what you or anyone else will earn.
