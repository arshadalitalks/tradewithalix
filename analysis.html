Four stages, every idea.

The same order each time. Most of the work happens before the entry, and plenty of ideas end without one.

1

Plan

Levels, bias and the conditions for an entry are marked before the session.

2

Wait

No entry until those conditions line up. Staying out is also a decision.

3

Execute

Stop and position size are set before the order goes in. Targets are staged in advance.

4

Manage and review

Let valid trades play out, then look back at what happened and why.

What Alix trades, and why.

Liquidity

Deep, active markets give tight spreads and clean fills during the main sessions.

Structure

Readable price action: levels you can mark before the session, not find after it.

Gold first

Every trade listed in the account history on the Performance page is XAUUSD.

Risk comes first.

Strategy matters, but risk decides whether a trader lasts. Losses are part of trading and are handled by rule.

Defined before entry

The stop is set before the order goes in. Size follows from the stop distance, never the other way round.

Patience on both sides

Wait for setups that line up. Once a valid trade is on, let it play out instead of interfering.

Limits

Risk per trade and drawdown limits are not published yet.

What to expect from us

  • No guaranteed outcomes
  • Results that can be traced to a source
  • No pressure to trade bigger
